## 1.0 Week Overview

Welcome to the start of your journey into Web3 development! This first week is the most critical, as we move beyond the industry buzzwords to establish a rigorous, technical understanding of the foundations that make Ethereum truly revolutionary. We will analyze the historical paradigm shift from the centralized internet (Web2) to the decentralized web (Web3). We will then dive deep into the core technical engine: Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT), the concepts of immutability and consensus, and the indispensable role of cryptography, specifically hashing and public-key infrastructure that secures every transaction and identity on the network. This foundation ensures that we understand *why* we build decentralized applications (dApps) the way we do, prioritizing security, ownership, and resilience.

## 1.1 Weekly Learning Objectives

By the end of this week, students will be able to:

1. **Articulate the Web3 Paradigm:** Compare and contrast Web2 and Web3 concerning decentralization, trust models, data ownership, and identity management.
2. **Explain Blockchain Mechanics:** Define DLT, explain how blocks are linked via cryptography, and articulate the concept of immutability and consensus mechanisms, including Byzantine Fault Tolerance (BFT).
3. **Apply Cryptographic Hashing:** Describe the properties of hash functions, differentiate between SHA-256 and Ethereum’s Keccak-256 standard, and implement basic hashing utilities in JavaScript/Node.js.
4. **Understand Key Management:** Explain the mathematical relationship between private keys, public keys, and Ethereum addresses, focusing on the role of Elliptic Curve Digital Signature Algorithm (ECDSA).
5. **Deconstruct Transactions:** Detail the lifecycle of an Externally Owned Account (EOA) transaction, explain the role of a digital signature (non-repudiation), and interpret the EIP-1559 gas fee structure (Base Fee, Tip, Burning).
